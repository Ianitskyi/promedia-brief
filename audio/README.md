# BRIEF sonic identity

BRIEF uses a **sound-only sonic identity**. No spoken brand lines are used in the intro, mid-roll bumper, or outro.

The system is built from one recognisable musical motif, adapted into three lengths.

## 1. Intro
Length: approximately 2.5–4 seconds.

- recognisable BRIEF motif
- instrumental only
- clean entrance into the briefing
- no voiceover

## 2. Mid-roll bumper
Length: approximately 1–1.5 seconds.

- shortest recognisable version of the same motif
- instrumental only
- used once near the natural midpoint of an 8–12 minute edition
- never interrupts a story

## 3. Outro
Length: approximately 3–4 seconds.

- resolved version of the same motif
- instrumental only
- clear sonic ending

## Sound direction

- contemporary news / public-radio feel
- calm, intelligent, recognisable
- minimal and editorial
- clear on phone speakers
- no cinematic drama
- no alarm-like sounds
- no generic corporate music
- no folk cliché
- no imitation of an existing broadcaster

## Current working assets

- intro: short version of the selected BRIEF Pulse motif
- bumper: approximately 1.2 seconds
- outro: approximately 3.7 seconds

## Product rule

The sonic identity should identify BRIEF without needing to say the brand name aloud. Spoken narration begins only after the intro sting.
