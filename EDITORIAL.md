# BRIEF editorial structure

## Core principle

BRIEF is not a chronological feed. It is a ranked daily briefing built from trusted Ukrainian journalism.

The edition should answer three questions:
1. What matters nationally today?
2. What matters in the regions today?
3. What can the listener explore next?

## Block 1 — National agenda

Start with the most important national stories of the day.

Typical categories:
- war and security
- politics and government decisions
- economy and business
- international developments affecting Ukraine
- society
- technology / AI / media when nationally relevant

The order is based on editorial significance, not publication time.

## Block 2 — Regions

A separate regional block follows the national agenda.

The purpose is not to include every oblast every day. Include only genuinely important regional stories.

Geographic scanning should cover the whole country, with deliberate attention to:
- frontline and near-frontline regions
- large cities / metropolitan areas
- central Ukraine
- western and rear regions
- Volyn
- Khmelnytskyi region
- Zakarpattia

A practical listening order may move from frontline/eastern regions toward western/rear regions, but ranking still follows importance.

### Regional importance signals

Use several signals together:
- number of independent eligible media covering the same event
- appearance in both regional and national media
- citation / pickup by other media
- evidence of broad public impact
- scale of consequences
- public-safety relevance
- government / emergency / infrastructure significance
- original reporting or exclusive information from a trusted regional newsroom

Do not expose the full ranking mechanics to users unless they ask.

Do not force geographic quotas. A region may be absent on a quiet day; another may have several stories if events justify it.

## Block 3 — Interaction

End every edition with a short invitation to continue:

**«Хочете дізнатися більше про будь-яку з цих тем — просто запитайте BRIEF.»**

The app should then offer simple paths such as:
- Про цю історію
- За темою
- За регіоном
- Що ще важливого сьогодні?

Answers must be grounded only in the trusted-source corpus and should link back to original journalism.

## Suggested 10-minute edition

- 0:00–0:03 — sonic intro
- 0:03–5:30 — national agenda
- ~5:30 — short sonic bumper
- 5:31–9:20 — regional agenda
- 9:20–9:50 — one useful “read/listen more” recommendation or context item
- 9:50–10:00 — interaction prompt + sonic outro

This timing is flexible. Importance outranks rigid duration.
